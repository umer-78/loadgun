Four decisions that decide whether the numbers mean anything

  • Percentiles are nearest-rank, not interpolated. p95 is a latency a request actually experienced.
  • A failed request has no latency. Timeouts stay out of the distribution.
  • Duration stops new requests; it does not cut in-flight ones.
  • The body is drained before the clock stops. keepalive measures the server, not the handshake.
